Impact & Change

Social-Cultural Impacts

  • Ondol was significant in reshaping the cultural identity of Korea; after the proliferation of Ondol, most Koreans spent time in thier house while sitting down the most of the times which this type of lifestyle in Korean was called 좌식문화
  • Since Ondol could keep the floors warm, Koreans started to have a lifestyle mostly based on the floors.
  • As an example, floor beddings were used instead of raised beds.
  • Confuciast customs: Also, the warmest rooms were reserved for elders and distinguished guests.
  • Koreans in the Chosun Dynasty widely adopted Ondol in many households.
  • Also, countries outside Korea use Ondol too.
  • As an example, a school in Bhutan uses Ondol to keep the students warm which shows that Ondol has affected people’s lives both inside and outside of Korea.

Technology Comparison Diagram

Furnace
  • Ondol developed from a furnace, which used burning charcoal to generate heat.
  • Initially, when people used furnaces, there were some inconvenient aspects of it, including the fact that furnaces couldn’t heat up the room evenly.
  • It was only able to heat up the areas near the fire.
Ondol System
  • Ondol was a new technology used to evenly heat up the floors.
  • The complex mechanism of Ondol helped people evenly heat up the floors in an efficient way.
  • Therefore, Koreans developed a new system called Ondol.
  • In an Ondol system, fire was created in a firebox called Agungi, and the hot gases traveled through underground channels known as Gorae.
  • People were able to have their rooms evenly heated with much more practical technology.
  • Because Ondol kept the floors warm, Koreans adopted a lifestyle centered on the floor.
  • For example, floor bedding was used instead of raised beds.
  • Also, the warmest rooms were reserved for elders and distinguished guests.
  • These examples demonstrate how Ondol transformed people’s lifestyles.
  • This shows a significant impact on Koreans’ daily lives.
