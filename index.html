Ondol: Revolutionary Heating System of South Korea

By Joon, Olive, Esther, and Jade

Historical Question

How has Ondol transformed Korea’s daily life, scientific aspects, and social customs, and what were its lasting impacts worldwide?

Thesis

Ondol was a unique scientific and cultural innovation in Korea, being a distinctive, complex floor heating system from an early period that shaped new customs in Korea. The Ondol has left a significant mark as a foundational heating system within the nation and prevails worldwide today.
