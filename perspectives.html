Ondol Forum

Goguryeo Commoners' Perspective

  • The Korean Ondol spread from the common people in the Goguryeo Kingdom, as they needed something to heat them during the cold winters of Korea.
  • Ondol kept the people warm throughout the winters, and it was a great innovation because of its extremely high accessibility.
  • The implementation of the Ondol affected common people by allowing and helping their survival during brutal winters.
  • “벽화무덤의 주인공들인 귀족들과 달리 고구려의 민중들은 ‘돈이 덜 드는 난방 방식’인 구들을 택하였음이 분명하다. '신당서’와 '구당서' 기사처럼 ‘가난한 사람들이’ 겨울을 나기 위해서는 구들이 필수적이었음직하다…”

Joseon Nobles' Perspective

  • Later, the nobles started to implement the Ondol, which was the common people’s custom, during approximately the Joseon Kingdom.
  • Unlike the common people, the Ondol didn’t necessarily allow the survival of nobles, as the nobles already had a heating system, the furnace.
  • Moreover, the nobles usually sat on chairs or some kind of furniture instead of the floor to prevent the cold.
  • However, as the Ondol was spread to nobles, their social customs began to change. The nobles started to set the tables with food laid out on the floor and eat on the floor as the Ondol was brought.
  • Sitting on floors became common even for dinners in banquets, and the Ondol started the Korean tradition of putting seat cushions (방석) on the floor and eating together.
  • “온돌의 보급은 생활 방식과 식사 문화도 변화하게 만들었다. 의자에 앉는 입식 생활이 바닥에 앉는 좌식 생활로 바뀌었다. 또 고려시대에 귀족은 의자에 앉아 식탁에 차려진 음식을 먹었는데, 조선시대에는 좌식이 퍼지면서 고급스러운 연회에서도 바닥에 앉아서 먹기 시작했다. 이에 따라 발이 낮은 소반이 유행했다.” (가스신문)

International Communities' Perspective

  • Today, Ondol is being brought to other countries too.
  • One example is the Laya Central School in Bhutan. The school has been using the heating system of Bukhari, which creates a lot of smoke and comes at a high cost.
  • However, they implemented the Korean Ondol, which “will ease all these challenges”, according to the source.
  • Like this case, other countries are being affected by the scientific and eco-friendly system of Ondol and are slowly bringing the Korean Ondol to their countries.
