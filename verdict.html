Conclusion — Historical Significance of Ondol

  • The development of Ondol was a scientific and cultural innovation in Korea that provided advanced floor heating in Korean houses from an early age and formed new customs within families.
  • The Ondol’s system of floor heating is still used very commonly in Korea and has spread to other countries, leaving a lasting impact worldwide after its development during the Goguryeo Kingdom.
